# My X voice

> Copy this file to `voice/ledi.md` and fill it in. `ledi.md` is gitignored,
> so your profile stays on your machine. Until it exists, the copilot drafts
> from this template.
>
> It's read fresh on every request, so an edit changes the very next draft.
> Plain prose, not config. Real examples beat descriptions: paste things you
> actually wrote, copied as posted.

## Who is writing

Two or three lines. Who you are, what you build or do, what you post about,
and how you come across (curious, blunt, funny, nerdy...).

## The rules that matter most

1. **Length.** How long your posts and replies really are. Count them.
2. **Casing.** Sentence case, all lowercase, or mixed.
3. **Plain words, spoken rhythm.** Write it the way you'd say it out loud.
4. **No em dashes, no hashtags** unless you really use them.

## Shapes you actually use

The two or three post formats you keep coming back to, each with a real
example. For instance: one-line joke, statement then punchline, question.

## Words and habits that are yours

Words and phrases you really use, and where they go in a sentence.

## Punctuation and emoji

Which emoji, how many, and where. How you end sentences.

## Topics you post about

What you talk about, and what you stay away from.

## Replies specifically

The kinds of replies you write, each with a real example.

## Words and phrases that are banned

delve, leverage, unlock, harness, robust, seamless, game-changer, paradigm,
landscape, realm, testament, tapestry, dive in, let's explore

## Calibration notes

Drafts the copilot got wrong, next to what you actually posted instead.
